Inama ku bucuruzi

Amakosa akunze gukorwa mu kwandika imyenda y'abakiriya

Menya ibibazo bikunze kubaho n'uko wanonosora uburyo bwo kubika inyandiko.

Iyo imyenda y'abakiriya itanditswe neza, umucuruzi ashobora kugorwa no kumenya amafaranga agomba kwishyurwa. Dore amakosa akunze kubaho n'uburyo bwo kuyirinda.

1. Kwishingikiriza ku kwibuka

Kwibuka si uburyo bwizewe bwo kubika inyandiko. Jya wandika buri gikorwa cy'ubucuruzi cyakozwe ku ideni.

2. Kwibagirwa kwandika ubwishyu

Buri bwishyu bugomba kwandikwa. Iyo utabyanditse, umwenda ushobora kugaragara nk'aho ukiri munini kurusha uko ungana.

3. Kuvanga amakuru y'abakiriya

Tandukanya inyandiko za buri mukiriya kugira ngo amafaranga y'umwe atitiranywa n'ay'undi.

4. Kutandika amatariki

Amatariki afasha kumenya igihe igikorwa cyangwa ubwishyu byabereye. Jya uyandika buri gihe.

5. Kutagenzura amafaranga asigaye

Jya usuzuma inyandiko zawe kandi uzigereranye n'inyemezabwishyu cyangwa ibindi bimenyetso bihari.

6. Gukwirakwiza amakuru y'ibanga

Amakuru y'imyenda y'abakiriya ni ay'ibanga. Yasangize gusa abantu bafite uburenganzira bwo kuyabona.

Shyiraho gahunda ihoraho

Hitamo uburyo bumwe bwo kwandika, buvugurure igihe habaye impinduka kandi ujye ubusuzuma. Porogaramu yo gukurikirana imyenda ishobora gufasha gutunganya iyi mirimo.
