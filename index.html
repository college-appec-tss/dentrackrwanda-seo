A practical business habit

Why accurate customer debt records matter

When a business sells goods or services on credit, it needs a reliable way to record each transaction. Without clear records, it can become difficult to remember payment dates, partial payments and outstanding amounts.

DenTrack Rwanda is intended to help make that record-keeping process more organised.

Read the debt tracking guide

A simple record-keeping routine

  1. Record the customer and transaction.
  2. Write down the amount owed.
  3. Record each payment received.
  4. Review the remaining balance.
  5. Keep records accurate and up to date.
