Who may need debt tracking?

Small shops, service providers and other businesses that allow customers to pay later may benefit from maintaining clear debt and payment records. The right record-keeping method depends on the size and needs of each business.

Why record partial payments?

A customer may pay a debt in more than one instalment. Recording each payment helps the business calculate and review the remaining balance more accurately.
