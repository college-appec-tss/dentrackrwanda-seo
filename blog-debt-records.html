How to Keep Accurate Customer Debt Records

A clear record helps a business understand transactions and payments.

Accurate customer debt records are important for businesses that allow customers to pay later. They provide a written history of what was supplied, what was owed and what has been paid.

What should a debt record include?

  • A customer name or reference.
  • The date of the transaction.
  • A description of the goods or services.
  • The original amount owed.
  • The date and amount of each payment.
  • The remaining balance.

Update records immediately

Record new transactions and payments as soon as practical. Delayed updates can lead to forgotten payments or incorrect balances.

Keep a consistent format

Use the same fields and process for each customer. Consistency makes it easier to review records and identify missing information.

Check balances

Compare the recorded balance with the original amount and all payments. If something does not match, investigate and correct the record with an explanation.

Protect records

Keep business records in a secure place. Do not give access to people who do not need customer financial information.
