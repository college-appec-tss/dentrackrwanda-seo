Inyigisho ku bucuruzi

Uko wakurikirana imyenda y'abakiriya mu bucuruzi

Intambwe zagufasha kwandika ibyo wagurishije ku ideni, ubwishyu n'amafaranga asigaye.

Iyo umucuruzi yemereye umukiriya kwishyura nyuma, aba agomba kwandika neza amafaranga umukiriya agomba kwishyura. Gukurikirana imyenda bifasha kumenya amafaranga amaze kwishyurwa n'asigaye.

1. Andika buri kintu cyagurishijwe ku ideni

Andika itariki, umukiriya, ibyo yaguze n'amafaranga agomba kwishyura.

2. Tunganya amakuru y'abakiriya

Koresha uburyo buhoraho bwo kumenya buri mukiriya. Ntukishingikirize gusa ku kwibuka cyangwa ku mpapuro zitatanye.

3. Andika buri bwishyu

Igihe umukiriya yishyuye, andika itariki n'amafaranga yishyuye. Niba yishyuye mu byiciro, andika buri bwishyu ukwabwo.

4. Reba amafaranga asigaye

Uburyo bworoshye bwo kubara ni: amafaranga asigaye = amafaranga yose yari agomba kwishyurwa − amafaranga yose amaze kwishyurwa.

5. Jya usuzuma inyandiko

Jya ureba inyandiko zawe buri gihe. Kosora amakosa ukimara kuyabona kandi ubike ibimenyetso by'ubwishyu igihe bihari.

6. Rinda amakuru y'abakiriya

Amakuru y'imyenda y'abakiriya ni amakuru y'ibanga. Yereke gusa abantu bemerewe kuyabona.

Gukoresha porogaramu yo gukurikirana imyenda

Porogaramu ishobora gufasha gutunganya amakuru y'imyenda n'ubwishyu. DenTrack Rwanda ni umushinga ugamije gufasha muri ubu buryo. Sura porogaramu urebe ibyo ishobora gukora ubu.
