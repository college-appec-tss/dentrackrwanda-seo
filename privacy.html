Important information about this website and the separate DenTrack Rwanda application.

About this information website

This website provides general information and educational content about customer debt and payment record management. It is separate from the DenTrack Rwanda application.

Information you provide

This information website does not intentionally ask visitors to enter customer debt details, passwords or payment information. If you use the separate application, review its privacy information and terms before entering personal or business information.

External links

This website links to the DenTrack Rwanda application hosted on another service. When you follow that link, the application and its hosting provider may process information according to their own policies.

Cookies and analytics

If analytics, advertising or other tracking technologies are added to this website, this page should be updated to identify the tools used, the information they collect and the choices available to visitors.

Keeping information safe

Do not publish passwords or private customer financial information on public pages. Use appropriate access controls when managing business records.

Updates

This privacy information may be updated when the website changes. Visitors should review this page periodically.

This is general website privacy information, not a substitute for a complete privacy policy for the DenTrack Rwanda application. Update it to reflect the actual data practices before launch.
