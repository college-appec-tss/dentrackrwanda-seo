Ibibazo bikunze kubazwa

Menya ibijyanye no gukurikirana imyenda no kwandika ubwishyu bw'abakiriya.

Gukurikirana umwenda bisobanura iki?

Ni uburyo bwo kwandika amafaranga umukiriya agomba kwishyura, ayo amaze kwishyura n'asigaye.

Kuki umucuruzi agomba kwandika ubwishyu?

Inyandiko z'ubwishyu zifasha kumenya amafaranga umukiriya amaze kwishyura n'asigaye.

Ubwishyu bw'igice ni iki?

Ni igihe umukiriya yishyuye igice cy'amafaranga yari agomba kwishyura. Amafaranga asigaye na yo agomba kwandikwa.

Ni bande bashobora gukenera porogaramu yo gukurikirana imyenda?

Abacuruzi bemerera abakiriya kwishyura nyuma bashobora gukenera uburyo bwo gukurikirana imyenda.

Ni hehe nabona amakuru y'ibiciro?

Sura porogaramu ya DenTrack Rwanda urebe amakuru agezweho ajyanye n'ibiciro n'ikoreshwa.

Natangira nte?

Fungura porogaramu ya DenTrack Rwanda ukurikize amabwiriza ahari yo gutangira.
