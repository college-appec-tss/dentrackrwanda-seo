Impamvu inyandiko z'imyenda ari ingenzi

Iyo ucuruza ku ideni, ni ngombwa kwandika neza ibyo umukiriya yatwaye, amafaranga agomba kwishyura n'ayo yishyuye. Iyo inyandiko zidahari cyangwa zituzuye, gukurikirana imyenda biragorana.

DenTrack Rwanda igamije gufasha mu gutunganya izi nyandiko.

Soma inyandiko ku myenda

Uburyo bwiza bwo kwandika imyenda

  1. Andika amazina y'umukiriya n'ibyo yaguze.
  2. Andika amafaranga agomba kwishyura.
  3. Andika buri bwishyu yakwishyuye.
  4. Reba amafaranga asigaye.
  5. Jya uvugurura inyandiko igihe cyose habaye impinduka.
