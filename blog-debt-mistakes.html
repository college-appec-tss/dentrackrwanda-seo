Common Customer Debt Record Mistakes to Avoid

Improve your record-keeping process by recognising common problems.

When customer debts are not recorded consistently, a business may find it difficult to confirm what is owed. Here are common mistakes and ways to improve the process.

1. Depending on memory

Memory is not a reliable record system. Write down each credit transaction instead of depending on remembering it later.

2. Forgetting to record payments

Every payment should be recorded. If a payment is missed, the balance may appear higher than the actual amount owed.

3. Mixing customer records

Keep each customer's transactions identifiable. Avoid combining separate customers' balances in one unclear note.

4. Not recording dates

Dates help explain when a transaction happened and when a payment was received. Include them consistently.

5. Failing to review balances

Review records regularly and compare them with available receipts or other supporting information.

6. Sharing private information carelessly

Customer debt information should be treated as private business information. Share it only with authorised people.

Build a better routine

Choose a consistent method, update it promptly and check it regularly. A debt tracking tool may help organise the process.
