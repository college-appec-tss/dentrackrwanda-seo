Menya DenTrack Rwanda

Umushinga ugamije gufasha gutunganya inyandiko z'imyenda n'ubwishyu bw'abakiriya.

DenTrack Rwanda ni iki?

DenTrack Rwanda ni umushinga wa porogaramu igamije gufasha abantu gutunganya amakuru ajyanye n'imyenda abakiriya babafitiye n'ubwishyu bakira.

Kuki ari ingenzi?

Abacuruzi bemerera abakiriya kwishyura nyuma bakenera kubika neza amakuru y'ibyo bagurishije, amafaranga bagomba kwishyurwa n'ayo bakiriye.

Icyo umushinga wibandaho

Umushinga wibanda ku gufasha gutunganya inyandiko z'imyenda no gutanga amakuru afasha abantu kunoza uburyo babika inyandiko z'ubucuruzi.
