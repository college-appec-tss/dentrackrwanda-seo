Gusuzuma amakuru

Ongera urebe amakuru wanditse igihe usuzuma konti z'abakiriya.

Ibyo porogaramu ishobora gukora bishobora guhinduka bitewe na verisiyo iriho. Sura porogaramu urebe ibikora ubu.

Ni bande bashobora gukenera gukurikirana imyenda?

Abacuruzi, amaduka n'abatanga serivisi bemerera abakiriya kwishyura nyuma bashobora gukenera uburyo bwo kubika neza inyandiko z'imyenda n'ubwishyu.

Kuki ari ngombwa kwandika ubwishyu bw'igice?

Umukiriya ashobora kwishyura umwenda mu byiciro. Kwandika buri bwishyu bituma umenya amafaranga asigaye neza.
