Frequently asked questions

Learn about debt tracking and keeping customer payment records.

What is debt tracking?

Debt tracking is the process of recording amounts owed, payments received and balances that remain unpaid.

Why should a business record customer payments?

Payment records help a business review how much a customer has paid and how much remains outstanding.

What is a partial payment?

A partial payment is when a customer pays only part of the amount owed. The remaining amount should still be recorded.

Who may need a debt tracking tool?

Businesses that allow customers to buy goods or services on credit may find a debt tracking tool useful.
