About DenTrack Rwanda

A project focused on making customer debt and payment record-keeping more organised.

What is DenTrack Rwanda?

DenTrack Rwanda is a debt tracking application project intended to help users organise information about customer debts and payments. It is designed around a common business need: keeping a clearer record of amounts owed and payments received.

Why does it matter?

Businesses that sell on credit need to keep track of transactions. Accurate records can help reduce confusion, support better communication with customers and make it easier to review outstanding balances.

Our focus

The project focuses on practical debt record management and accessible information for people interested in improving how they keep business records.
