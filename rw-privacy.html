Amakuru yerekeye ibanga

Amakuru y'ingenzi yerekeye uru rubuga na porogaramu ya DenTrack Rwanda.

Ibyerekeye uru rubuga

Uru rubuga rutanga amakuru rusange n'inyigisho zijyanye no gutunganya imyenda n'ubwishyu bw'abakiriya. Rutandukanye na porogaramu ya DenTrack Rwanda.

Amakuru utanga

Uru rubuga ntirusaba ko ushyiraho amakuru y'imyenda y'abakiriya, amagambo y'ibanga cyangwa amakuru yo kwishyura. Niba ukoresha porogaramu itandukanye, banza usome amabwiriza yayo yerekeye ibanga.

Imiyoboro ijya ku zindi mbuga

Uru rubuga rufite umurongo ugana kuri porogaramu ya DenTrack Rwanda iri ahandi. Iyo uyikandagiye, amakuru yawe ashobora gukoreshwa hakurikijwe amabwiriza y'urwo rubuga na serivisi iyicumbikiye.

Cookies n'isesengura ry'abasura

Niba hongerwamo ibikoresho byo gusesengura abasura cyangwa kwamamaza, uru rupapuro rugomba kuvugururwa rukavuga ibyo bikoresho n'amakuru bishobora gukusanya.

Kubika amakuru mu mutekano

Ntushyire ku mugaragaro amagambo y'ibanga cyangwa amakuru y'ibanga y'abakiriya. Abemerewe gusa ni bo bagomba kubona amakuru y'ubucuruzi.

Aya ni amakuru rusange yerekeye uru rubuga. Agomba kuvugururwa kugira ngo ahuze n'uburyo nyakuri porogaramu ya DenTrack Rwanda ikusanya kandi ikoresha amakuru.
